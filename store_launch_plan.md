## ⚡ 2. Jamaica-Specific "Need-to-Haves" (Critical Infrastructure)

Operating a tech and gaming business in Jamaica has unique operational requirements:

### A. Power Stability & Climate Control
1. **Commercial Inverter Air Conditioning (AC):**
   - High BTU capacity (e.g., dual 24,000–36,000 BTU inverter split units).
   - *Why:* High tropical humidity damages raw cardboard/foils (warping/curling) and heat generated by 10+ PCs, TVs, and 30+ bodies will overheat the venue quickly.
2. **Heavy-Duty Online UPS (Uninterruptible Power Supply) & Surge Protectors:**
   - Pure sine wave UPS units for all PC setups, POS register, and network rack.
   - *Why:* JPS power fluctuations, voltage spikes, and brief dropouts will ruin PC hardware and disconnect live competitive matches.
3. **Backup Power / Generator Transfer Switch:**
   - Generator or hybrid inverter battery system to keep lights, Wi-Fi, POS, and basic tournament operation alive during blackouts.

### B. High-Speed Internet & Failover
1. **Primary High-Speed Fiber Line:** (Flow or Digicel Business Fiber, minimum 150–300 Mbps symmetrical with low ping to US East servers like Miami/Virginia).
2. **Secondary LTE / Backup Connection:** Dual-WAN router (e.g., Ubiquiti UniFi Dream Machine or TP-Link Omada) configured for seamless failover so online tournament pairing and PC matches don't get interrupted.

### C. Security & Loss Prevention
1. **Multi-Camera 4K/2K CCTV System:**
   - Overheads covering: Cash register, glass showcases (singles), entry/exit, tournament floor, and PC stations.
2. **Secure Safe / Fortified Storage:**
   - For high-value raw/graded singles and sealed booster cases.
3. **Commercial Storefront Security:**
   - Roller shutters / grille gate and commercial alarm monitoring.

## 📦 3. Equipment & Purchasing Master List

### 🃏 A. Retail & TCG Tournament Equipment
- [ ] **Glass Display Showcases (Counter Height):** 2 to 3 units with sliding lockable glass doors, velvet display trays, and internal LED lighting for singles.
- [ ] **Slatwall / Gridwall Panels:** For hanging card sleeves (Dragon Shield, Katana, KMC), deck boxes, binders, and playmats.
- [ ] **6-Foot Heavy-Duty Folding Banquet Tables:** (Plastic or wooden top with smooth finish). Standard size: 72" x 30".
- [ ] **Commercial Ergonomic Stackable Chairs:** (Plastic/metal, padded if budget permits) — minimum 30–50 chairs.
- [ ] **Tournament Supplies:**
  - Table number stands (1–30+).
  - Dry-erase tournament bracket board or wall-mounted 50"+ TV for round pairings and tournament timer.
  - Large digital countdown clock / timer.
  - Basic playmats for store loan/feature table.
  - Card accessories stock: Penny sleeves, top loaders, semi-rigids, team bags, deck boxes, 9-pocket/12-pocket binders, dice sets, damage counters.

### 🖥️ B. PC Gaming Setup (Target: 6 to 10 Stations)
- [ ] **Gaming PCs:**
  - **CPU:** AMD Ryzen 5 7600X / Intel Core i5-13400 or newer.
  - **GPU:** NVIDIA RTX 4060 / 4060 Ti (handles all popular esports: League of Legends, Valorant, Apex, Fortnite, Overwatch, Master Duel, Steam games at high FPS).
  - **RAM:** 32 GB DDR5.
  - **Storage:** 1TB–2TB NVMe SSD.
  - **Case/Cooling:** Good mesh airflow cases with dust filters.
- [ ] **Monitors:** 24" to 27" 1080p/1440p, 144Hz–240Hz IPS gaming monitors with 1ms response time.
- [ ] **Peripherals:**
  - Mechanical keyboards (Hot-swappable/durable switches).
  - Gaming mice (e.g., Logitech G502 / Razer DeathAdder).
  - Heavy-duty gaming headsets with noise-isolating mics.
  - XL/XXL desk pads.
- [ ] **Gaming LAN Desks & Chairs:** Sturdy steel-frame desks with integrated cable management; durable ergonomic high-back chairs.
- [ ] **Cafe Management Software:** (e.g., *Smartlaunch*, *Gizmo*, *Senet*, or *CyberCafePro*) to track user hours, auto-lock screens, and manage rates.

### 🎮 C. Pokémon VGC & Console Stations
- [ ] **Console Monitors:** 24"–27" 1080p 60Hz/144Hz low input lag gaming monitors.
- [ ] **Official Nintendo Switch Docks & Power Adapters:** Pre-wired to HDMI monitors with accessible USB ports for wired controllers (Pro Controllers / GameCube adapters).
- [ ] **HDMI Switchers / Splitters:** For easy swapping between consoles or mirroring matches to spectator TVs.

### 💼 D. Point of Sale (POS) & Administration
- [ ] **POS Hardware:** iPad / Android Tablet stand + cash drawer + thermal receipt printer + barcode scanner.
- [ ] **POS Software:**
  - *Square* / *Shopify POS* (great general retail + inventory).
  - *BinderPOS* / *TCGplayer Pro* / *RokitPOS* (specifically designed for TCG singles and sealed box inventory).
- [ ] **Label & Barcode Printer:** (e.g., Dymo 450/550 or Brother QL-800 for tagging singles with set numbers and prices).

## 🏆 4. Tournament Management & Distributor Access

To run official sanctioned events and purchase product at wholesale prices:

### Distributor Accounts
- Apply for accounts with major US / LATAM distributors:
  - **GTS Distribution, ACD Distribution, PeachState Monster, Southern Hobby, Grosnor, Coqui Hobby (Caribbean/LATAM).**
- *Logistics:* Setup a reliable Miami-based freight forwarding partner (e.g., air freight for high-demand release day stock, sea freight/ocean shipping for heavy sealed cases and tournament furniture).

### Official Tournament Organizer (TO) Platforms
1. **Konami OTS (Official Tournament Store):**
   - Register through Konami Card Game Network (KCGN). Uses the **Konami NEURON** app for pairings.
   - OTS status unlocks exclusive OTS Tournament Packs, sneak peeks, and official playmats.
2. **Bandai TCG+:**
   - For *One Piece Card Game*, *Digimon*, *Dragon Ball Super*, *Union Arena*.
   - All events, pre-releases, and promo kits are managed via the **Bandai TCG+ app**.
3. **Play! Pokémon (Pokémon TCG & VGC):**
   - Apply for the **Professor Program** to become a certified Tournament Organizer / Judge.
   - Apply for **Play! Pokémon Store** status to get Pre-Release Kits, League Promos, and sanctioned Championship Points (CP) events.
4. **Wizards Play Network (WPN) for MTG:**
   - Manage events through **Wizards EventLink** and the **Magic Companion** app.
   - Progress from WPN to WPN Premium for exclusive commander parties and promotional allocations.
5. **Riftbound & Indie TCGs / General Software:**
   - Use **Challonge**, **Matcherino**, **Battlefy**, or **Melee.gg** for community-run events, custom formats, and PC/VGC brackets.

### Key Modules to Plan For:
1. **TCG Singles Catalog & Sync Engine:**
   - Integration with pricing APIs (Scryfall for MTG, YGOProDeck for Yu-Gi-Oh!, Pokémon TCG API, TCGplayer API).
   - Real-time stock status (Sync between in-store POS and online website).
2. **Tournament Registration & Ticketing:**
   - Players can browse weekly events (e.g., "Friday Night Magic", "Saturday Yu-Gi-Oh! OTS", "Sunday Pokémon VGC Cup").
   - Online seat reservation and ticket payment to lock in entry fees before caps are reached.
3. **PC Station Reservation:**
   - Book PC LAN slots by the hour or day-pass ahead of time.
4. **Payment Processing in Jamaica:**
   - **Local Currency (JMD):** WiPay Jamaica, NCB e-Commerce Gateway, First Atlantic Commerce (FAC).
   - **USD / International:** Stripe / PayPal / Shopify Payments.
5. **Tech Stack Recommendation:**
   - **Option A (Fastest to Market):** Shopify + BinderPOS integration (Turnkey solution for card singles, inventory, and POS sync).
   - **Option B (Custom Modern Web App):** Next.js / React frontend + Supabase / PostgreSQL backend + Stripe/WiPay API (Maximum customization, low ongoing subscription fees, bespoke tournament & PC booking engine).
