# 🇯🇲 Step-by-Step Execution, Inventory, & Promotional Plan

This document outlines the exact chronological steps to launch your TCG and Gaming Store in Jamaica, a master shopping list categorized by how to source them, and a localized promotional strategy.

---

## 🗺️ PART 1: Step-by-Step Execution Plan

### Phase 1: Legal & Logistics (Months 1-2)
1. **Business Registration:** Register your business name or LLC at the **Companies Office of Jamaica (COJ)**.
2. **Tax Compliance:** Obtain your Business TRN and a Tax Compliance Certificate (TCC) from the TAJ. You will need this for commercial banking and importing.
3. **Banking & Payments:** Open a local business bank account (NCB, Scotia, or Sagicor). Apply for a POS machine and explore local e-commerce gateways like WiPay for your future website.
4. **Setup Freight Forwarding:** Establish a commercial account with a Miami-to-Jamaica freight forwarder (e.g., Mailpac, Aeropost, Tara Courier, or a sea-freight consolidator for heavy items like tables and PCs). Your Miami address is crucial for US distributors.
5. **Apply for Distributors:** Use your registered business docs and Miami shipping address to apply to TCG distributors (GTS Distribution, PeachState, Southern Hobby, Coqui Hobby).

### Phase 2: Location & Infrastructure (Months 3-4)
1. **Secure a Location:** Sign a lease. Prioritize locations with safe parking and good foot traffic (plazas in Kingston/St. Andrew, Portmore, or Montego Bay).
2. **Security First:** Install physical grilles/shutters. Contract a local security firm (Hawkeye, KingAlarm) for an alarm system and install 4K CCTV cameras covering all angles.
3. **Internet & Power:** 
   - Apply for **Flow Business** or **Digicel Business** fiber internet. 
   - Consult an electrician to install dedicated circuits for the PC gaming LAN and AC units. 
   - Purchase and install pure sine wave UPS units to combat JPS power fluctuations.
4. **Climate Control:** Install high-BTU Inverter AC units. (Buy these locally to get warranties).

### Phase 3: Fit-Out & Setup (Month 5)
1. **Furniture & Fixtures:** Source glass display cases (often cheaper to have a local Jamaican glassworker/carpenter build custom showcases than to import them).
2. **Tech Installation:** Build/setup the PC LAN stations, Nintendo Switch docks, and network switches. Cable manage everything safely.
3. **Stock the Store:** Place your opening orders for sealed booster boxes, card sleeves, deck boxes, and snacks.

### Phase 4: Soft Launch & Official Registration (Month 6)
1. **Soft Opening:** Open to a small group of friends and local community leaders to stress-test the internet, AC, and point-of-sale system.
2. **Register as Official TO:** Apply for Konami OTS (Yu-Gi-Oh), Bandai TCG+, and Play! Pokémon using photos of your fully built, operational store.

## 📣 PART 3: Jamaican Promotional & Marketing Plan

In Jamaica, word-of-mouth, WhatsApp, and visual social media drive culture. Here is how to market your store:

### 1. Pre-Launch (Generate Hype)
- **The "WhatsApp Community":** Create a WhatsApp Group or Broadcast list immediately. Jamaican gamers coordinate heavily on WhatsApp. Tease behind-the-scenes photos of the store being built.
- **Instagram & TikTok (Visuals):** Post Reels/TikToks of the PC builds, unboxing new card stock, and store setup. Use popular local trending audio mixed with gaming/anime music.
- **Tap into Local Conventions:** Set up a small booth or hand out flyers at local events like *AnimeCom Jamaica* or local university tech/gaming club meetups (UWI, UTech).

### 2. The Grand Opening Weekend (The Hook)
- **Free Entry Tournaments:** Host a "Grand Opening Cup" for Yu-Gi-Oh!, Smash Bros, or Pokemon with free entry and store credit/product as prizes.
- **Influencer/Streamer Invites:** Invite local Jamaican gaming streamers, tech reviewers, or anime content creators to stream from your PC LAN setup on opening day.
- **Giveaways:** "First 20 people through the door get a free pack of sleeves or a booster pack."
- **Food & Vibe:** Have good music playing and maybe partner with a local food vendor or patty shop for opening day food.

### 3. Post-Launch & Retention (Keeping them coming back)
- **Consistent Weekly Schedule:** (e.g., Fighting Game Fridays, Yu-Gi-Oh Saturdays, Pokemon Sundays). Reliability is key.
- **Student Discounts:** Offer a 10% discount on PC LAN time for high school or university students showing ID.
- **Loyalty Program:** A digital punch card or POS-integrated loyalty system (e.g., "Buy 10 booster packs, get 1 free" or "Play 5 hours on PC, get 1 hour free").
- **Feature Matches on YouTube:** Record the finals of your weekly local tournaments and upload them with Jamaican commentary. It builds local stars and makes players want to return to be on camera.
